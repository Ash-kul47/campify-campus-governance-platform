🚀 What We Have Built – “Campify”
🔹 1. Core Idea

Campify is a structured, semi-anonymous campus grievance management platform.

It bridges two problems:

Students feel unheard.

Administration lacks prioritization clarity.

We built a system that:

Encourages student participation (like Reddit-style identity).

Preserves anonymity.

Provides data-driven prioritization for administrators.

Backend
